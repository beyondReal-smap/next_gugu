🗑️ Data Deletion

Delete Your Account and Data

Effective: October 22, 2026 · Based on the Korean version effective October 19, 2026

In Gugu Adventure, you can use every learning feature as a guest without signing in. If you have not linked an email, no learning records are stored on our server, so you can delete all of your data yourself, right away.

At a glance
  • If you only used the app as a guest — reset your records in the app or delete the app, and you’re done.
  • If you linked an email — delete it right away in the app under Profile → Settings → Protect Your Records → Delete Account.

1.Delete data on your device (immediately)

Data stored on your device, such as learning records, levels and achievements, can be deleted right away in either of two ways.

  • Reset in the app: Profile → Settings → Reset learning records — learning data is deleted immediately.
  • Delete the app: Deleting the app from your device also deletes all of its data on the device.

If you have not linked an email, there is no copy on our server, so deleted data cannot be recovered.

2.Account and learning records stored on our server

For accounts where a parent has linked an email and a Premium purchase has been confirmed, learning records are stored on our server. See Section 1 of the Privacy Policy for what is stored.

  • When you request account deletion, the account and the learning records stored on our server are deleted.
  • Purchase receipts are kept after being unlinked from the account, for refunds and audits. Receipts do not contain your name or email.
  • Deleting the app does not automatically delete server records. If you want them deleted, please make a request as described below.

App notification information

  • If you turned on app notifications, the information needed to send them (notification token, notification installation identifier, app version and language, notification settings, and records of consent to promotional messages) is stored on our server.
  • Without deleting your account, you can turn off all notifications under Profile → Settings → Notifications, and the notification information is deleted immediately. It is also deleted automatically if the app is not used for 270 days or more.
  • When you delete your account, notification information and records of consent to promotional messages are deleted as well.

3.Delete right in the app

Profile → Settings → Protect Your Records → Delete Account

Confirm once more, and the account and the learning records stored on our server are deleted immediately. Learning data on the device and your Lifetime Pass remain, and you can use the Lifetime Pass again with Restore Purchases.

4.Request by email

Contact email: admin@smap.site

Put “[Gugu Adventure] Account deletion request” in the subject line and send it from the email address linked in the app. This is how we confirm it’s you; we don’t need any other information.

We handle requests without delay and reply with the result within 30 days at the latest.

5.Purchase records

  • Premium purchase records are kept in the Apple App Store / Google Play account used for payment and are subject to each company’s policies. The Operator cannot delete these records.
  • Restoring purchases uses the records in your store account, so you can restore Premium with the same store account even after deleting your account.
