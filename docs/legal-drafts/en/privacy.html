Gugu Adventure Privacy Policy

Effective: [TBD on publication] · Based on the Korean version effective October 19, 2026

정진 (the “Operator,” “we,” “us”) complies with applicable data protection laws and does its best to keep your personal information safe. This policy applies to the mobile app and website Gugu Adventure (구구 어드벤처, the “Service”). The Service was previously named “구구단 마스터” (Gugudan Master); it is the same service regardless of the name change.

Key points
  • Signing in is optional. You can use every learning feature as a guest without signing in, and in that case we do not collect personal information. However, if a guest turns on app notifications, we collect the device information needed to send them (such as a notification token).
  • We send app notifications (Announcements and Weekly progress for parents) only if you turn them on yourself. Promotional notifications (“Events & offers”) are not offered or sent outside Korea (see Section 7).
  • Learning progress (levels, streaks, mastery, etc.) is stored only on your device by default and is not sent to our servers.
  • Only if you want to keep your records when changing devices can a parent link an email address; only then are learning records stored on our server. If no email is linked, records never leave the device.
  • Payments are made only through in-app purchase on the Apple App Store / Google Play. We do not store card numbers or other payment details.
  • The iOS and Android apps contain no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for behavioral (targeted) advertising.

2.Why we use your information

  • Account identification and providing the Service: processing email sign-in and keeping you signed in
  • Learning-record backup: letting you continue your records after changing devices or reinstalling the app (accounts with a linked email only)
  • Purchase management: confirming and restoring Premium, recognizing purchases on the web and other devices
  • Service improvement: improving features and finding errors by analyzing usage statistics (website only, via Google Analytics)
  • Customer support: handling inquiries and requests, and giving notice of announcements
  • Sending app notifications: update and maintenance announcements, Weekly progress for parents (a notice that the weekly learning report is ready), and account or Lifetime Pass notices
  • Promotional messages (Korea only): only for users in Korea who separately consented to “Events & offers” notifications — news about new features, events and discounts, and keeping records of that consent. Not offered outside Korea (see Section 7).

3.How long we keep your information

  • Account information (email, etc.): kept until you delete your account, then destroyed without delay.
  • Learning records stored on our server: deleted together with the account when you delete your account.
  • Purchase / Premium history: unlinked from the account when you delete your account, and kept for the period required by applicable law where retention is required. Receipts do not contain your name or email.
  • App notification information: destroyed without delay when you turn off all notifications in the app, when the app has not been used for 270 days or more, or when you delete your account. Records of consent to and withdrawal from promotional notifications are kept until account deletion to verify consent.
  • Automatically collected information (website): destroyed when the purpose of collection is achieved or according to Google Analytics’ data retention policy.

However, where laws require retention (for example, the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce), we keep the information for the required period.

7.App notifications and promotional messages

  • Types of notifications: (1) Announcements (updates, maintenance, etc.) (2) Weekly progress for parents (a notice that the weekly learning report is ready; only for parent accounts with record backup turned on) (3) Practice Reminder (scheduled only on the device; no information is sent to our server) (4) Events & offers (promotional; Korea only).
  • Optional: We send notifications only if you turn them on, and all features of the Service work without notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.
  • Local time: Weekly progress for parents is sent according to the time zone of your device, so that it arrives at a reasonable local time. This is why we store your device’s language and time zone.
  • No promotional notifications outside Korea: The option to receive Events & offers (promotional) notifications is shown only on devices using the Korean App Store storefront. Outside Korea the option is not shown, no promotional consent is requested, and we do not send promotional or marketing notifications. Allowing notifications on your device is never treated as consent to promotional messages.
  • If you consented in Korea and then moved: the Events & offers switch stays visible so that you can withdraw consent at any time under Profile → Settings → Notifications → Events & offers (promotional), free of charge.
  • Notification content: Notifications do not include a child’s learning figures (number of correct answers, accuracy, etc.), so these are not shown on the lock screen.

8.Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information.

  • Delete records on the device: Profile → Settings → Reset learning records immediately deletes learning data stored on the device. Deleting the app also deletes it.
  • Delete your account in the app: You can delete it yourself under Profile → Settings → Protect Your Records → Delete Account. This removes the account and the learning records stored on our server and unlinks your purchase history. Learning data on the device and your Lifetime Pass remain.
  • Request by email: You can also send a request from the linked email address to admin@smap.site. We handle requests without delay and reply with the result within 30 days at the latest. See the Account & Data Deletion guide for details.
  • Other rights: Requests for access, correction or suspension of processing can also be sent to the contact above, and we will act on them without delay.

Additional rights for users in specific regions are described in Sections 14 to 17.

9.How we destroy personal information

We destroy personal information without delay when the retention period ends or the purpose of processing is achieved. Information in electronic files is permanently deleted in a way that cannot be recovered or reproduced, and data stored on the device is deleted when the app is deleted or its data is reset.

10.Security measures

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encrypted storage of authentication information (such as sign-in sessions)
  • Minimizing and controlling access rights to personal information

11.Children’s privacy

The Service is a times-tables learning service for children and is available to all ages. Every learning feature can be used as a guest without signing in, so children can use the Service without providing personal information. If we learn that a child’s personal information was collected without the required consent, we will delete it without delay.

The feature that stores learning records on our server works only for accounts where a parent has linked an email and purchased the Lifetime Pass. Entering the email and making the purchase are intended to be done by a parent, and our server rejects learning-record uploads from accounts that do not meet these conditions.

However, a purchase record alone cannot confirm that the buyer is an adult (for example, Family Sharing or gift cards). The Service has no separate adult-verification step. To withdraw consent, request account deletion as described in Section 8.

App notifications do not include a child’s learning figures (number of correct answers, accuracy, etc.), so these are not shown on the lock screen. Promotional notifications are not offered outside Korea.

Region-specific rules for children’s data are described in Section 14 (United States — COPPA) and Section 15 (EEA/UK — GDPR). Korea-specific rules (the under-14 consent rule) are summarized in Section 17.
