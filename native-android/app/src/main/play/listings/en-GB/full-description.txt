Times tables become an adventure!

Gugu Adventure turns learning the 2–9 times tables into a game. Explore a 3D world, take on friendly characters in multiplication battles, and build a one-minute-a-day maths habit.

■ 3D Adventure — learn by exploring
· 8 regions, from Sprout Meadow to Lava Volcano — one for each times table
· Roam the world with a joystick and battle the residents with times tables
· Answer in a row to build combos! Beat the boss to open the next region

■ 6 game modes
· Practice: no pressure — missed problems come back later
· Speed Run: 10 problems at top speed
· 60-Second Challenge: score as many as you can before time runs out
· Survival: only 3 chances — how far can you go?
· Missing Number: multiplication in reverse — find the number behind the ?
· True or False: is the equation right? Decide fast

■ Progress you can see
· Streaks for practicing every day
· A Daily Goal ring that fills as you play
· Levels and XP for correct, speedy answers
· Mastery stars for every table from 2 to 9
· 17 achievements to collect

■ Mistakes are okay
See the right answer right away, and tricky problems come back just when you might forget them. "Retry mistakes" at the end of each round locks in weak spots.

■ Listen & Learn
A friendly teacher's voice reads every problem and the whole times table aloud — built into the app, so it works offline.
